Offer-static prompt rules

1. Use the physical product exactly as supplied in the reference images. Do not invent or replace branding on packaging.
2. The serum is a free gift only in the bundles where the offer specifies it.
3. The eye balm is included only in the highest bundle tier.
4. Human reference photos demonstrate mask texture/application and product handling; they are not mandatory casting references.
5. Make people-led ads feel native, everyday, slightly imperfect, tactile, and believable rather than polished AI studio work.
6. Hebrew visible in the ad must be copied exactly from the prompt. No spelling, punctuation, wording, or gender-form improvisation.
7. CTA must express a benefit or relief from a pain point, never generic wording such as 'להכיר את המסכה'.
